# Editorial Rubric

This is a subjective editorial index, not a measurement of SEO performance, factual truth, traffic or business outcomes. Score only the actual reviewed revision. Evidence defects are reported separately and cannot be canceled by a high score.

| Dimension | Weight | What to assess against the author brief |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience and purpose | 20% | Reader fit, thesis, intended takeaway and relevant positioning. |
| Technical explanation and reasoning | 20% | Mechanisms, failures, tradeoffs and their connection to the argument. |
| Evidence discipline | 20% | Traceability, bounded claims, hypothetical versus actual work, justified opening and conclusion. |
| Structure and reader comprehension | 15% | Coherent progression, useful headings and standalone readability. |
| Voice and specificity | 15% | Author fit, concrete language, absence of padding, preservation of accepted choices. |
| Practical usefulness | 10% | Insight or application appropriate to this article's purpose; no forced CTA. |

Score each dimension from 0–10. Shared anchors: 0–2 = absent or seriously defective; 3–4 = major gaps; 5–6 = useful with material weaknesses; 7–8 = strong with identifiable improvements; 9–10 = unusually clear and well supported for the stated purpose. Add one concrete reason per score. Total out of 100 = sum(score / 10 × weight). Do not change weights silently to improve a total or invent precision from multiple simulated reviewers.

A limited article-only review cannot assess evidence discipline: use `not assessed` for that dimension, report other dimension scores without an overall score, and state the limitation. No critical factual findings can be ruled out merely by a fluent article or an upstream persona review.

When SEO is requested, separately assess title clarity, topic focus, descriptive headings and apparent search-intent fit. Distinguish these observations from unmeasured demand, rankings, crawlability and conversion. Do not claim live-source verification unless it occurred; source presence alone is not verification.
