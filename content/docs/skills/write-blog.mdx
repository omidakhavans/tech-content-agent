# write-blog

## Purpose

Turns `context-brief.md` into a readable technical article without inventing experience, results, or unsupported claims.

## Inputs and outputs

Input is `context-brief.md` plus the article-specific author context. Output is the canonical `blog-draft.md`; `draft-notes.md` may record decisions and caveats.

## Relationships

It follows `build-evidence-context` and precedes `review-blog`. Use `edit-blog` for revisions instead of creating competing final filenames.
