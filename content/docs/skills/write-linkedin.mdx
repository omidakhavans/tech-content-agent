# write-linkedin

## Purpose

Adapts the reviewed canonical article for LinkedIn without independently researching or reconstructing the story.

## Inputs and outputs

Input is `blog-draft.md` and a matching, passing `blog-review.md`; `context-brief.md` is consulted only when needed. Output is `linkedin-draft.md` with status `draft_for_review`.

## Relationships

It runs after `review-blog` and shares its evidence boundary. It never publishes or calls LinkedIn APIs.
