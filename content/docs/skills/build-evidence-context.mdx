# build-evidence-context

## Purpose

Bridges research and writing. It merges `research-report.md` and `resources-report.md`, removes duplication, preserves traceability, and marks verified evidence, interpretation, unsupported claims, and unknowns.

## Inputs and outputs

Input is one or both research reports in a subject folder. Output is `context-brief.md`, optionally with `claim-map.md`, `excluded-evidence.md`, or `unknowns.md` when useful.

## Relationships

It is the only intended input to `write-blog`. It does not write a blog, social post, or final narrative.
